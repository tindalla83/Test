Approach

Transparent inputs. Defensible conclusions.

Viability disputes are rarely about the model — they are about the assumptions. We set ours out plainly, evidence every one, and stress-test the scheme before anyone else gets the chance to.

  1. Scoping

    A short call to establish the planning position, the decision you need to make, and the timescale you need it in.

  2. Evidence gathering

    Comparable land transactions, current build cost data, sales and rental evidence, and the relevant policy and cost plan inputs.

  3. Appraisal & sensitivity

    Residual land value and profit-led appraisals, run across sensitivity and scenario ranges rather than a single point answer.

  4. Report & negotiation

    A clear written assessment you can submit — then we sit at the table and defend it through to determination or appeal.

Why clients instruct us

A specialist practice, not a service line

Viability is all we do. That focus means we know how each authority's officers and their retained reviewers approach an appraisal, where the arguments usually turn, and which assumptions are worth fighting for.

  • Both sides of the table. Experience acting for applicants and for local planning authorities keeps our positions realistic.
  • Appraisal transparency. Full model handover on request, with every input traceable to its source.
  • Deliverability first. Advice framed around what will actually get built and funded, not the number that reads best.
  • Direct engagement. The person who runs your appraisal is the person who negotiates it and, if needed, gives evidence on it.
