How this maps to the workbook. Yellow cells are inputs, exactly as in Strat_Land_Concept_Viab_Res_Land_v6.0 (Freehold).xlsm. Everything else is calculated live: the unit calculator, the development appraisal, the residual land value, the 229‑month cashflow, interest, IRR and NPV all recalculate on every keystroke. Figures reconcile to the workbook to the penny on its shipped assumptions.

Two differences worth knowing. Interest is solved directly from the cashflow rather than pasted in by goal seek, so Cost of Finance is always current. And the workbook's XIRR reads a fixed 197‑month window; that is reproduced as the default so numbers tie out, with a switch to run IRR across the full cashflow.
